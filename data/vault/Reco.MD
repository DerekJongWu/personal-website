## Summary
Reco is a B2B cybersecurity company founded in 2020 and headquartered in New York City, with R&D operations in Israel. The company provides a Dynamic SaaS Security platform that pairs an extensive knowledge graph mapping of third-party services, identities, data flows, and AI agents to deliver real-time visibility, governance, and threat detection across the entire SaaS ecosystem — including shadow AI and the rapidly expanding long tail of AI-powered applications. Reco supports over 215 SaaS integrations and serves multiple Fortune 500 companies across financial services, healthcare, pharma, technology, and manufacturing.

## Problem
Enterprise SaaS ecosystems have grown far beyond what traditional security tools can monitor or protect, and the rapid infusion of AI is creating entirely new categories of ungoverned risk that are already being exploited in high-profile breaches.

* The average enterprise now runs over 1,000 SaaS applications — a 26% increase in just two years — with new apps constantly being added, updated, and forming SaaS-to-SaaS connections that security teams cannot track manually
* Eighty percent of employees admit to using unauthorized SaaS and AI applications ("shadow tools"), and 71% of knowledge workers are using AI tools without IT approval. This shadow AI and shadow SaaS contributes to an estimated 35% of data breaches
* AI is no longer a discrete tool that can be blocked — it is embedded within existing SaaS platforms, deployed as autonomous AI agents with their own identities and access rights, and connected to sensitive business systems through integrations that create new attack surfaces
* Legacy SaaS Security Posture Management (SSPM) tools were built for a static world of a few dozen core applications. They cannot keep pace with the proliferation of long-tail applications, AI agents, and the dynamic nature of modern SaaS-to-SaaS data flows
* Attackers are increasingly targeting smaller, less-mature SaaS applications that are deeply connected to core systems but lack robust security controls — using these long-tail apps as indirect entry points to sensitive data
* Security teams are drowning in scattered alerts without business context: understanding who accessed what, through which application, with what permissions, and whether it represents a real threat requires correlating identity, data, applications, agents, and access paths into a single model

## Product
Reco provides a Dynamic SaaS Security platform that secures the entire SaaS lifecycle through four integrated capabilities, powered by a proprietary Knowledge Graph and AI Agents:

* Application Discovery — Instantly discovers all SaaS applications, SaaS-to-SaaS connections, shadow SaaS, AI agents, and shadow AI tools, including their users and data flows. The platform's discovery engine can identify and classify over 50,000 applications. A patented SaaS App Factory™ enables integration with any new application within two to three days — maintaining industry-leading coverage as customers' SaaS ecosystems evolve
* Posture Management & Compliance (SSPM++) — Keeps security posture and compliance airtight with business context, even as applications and AI agents are continuously added and updated. Supports automated compliance monitoring against enterprise security policies
* Identity & Access Governance — Ensures accounts are always secure (e.g., with MFA) and access privileges are kept to a minimum across the SaaS ecosystem. Manages the full identity lifecycle including offboarding and deprovisioning
* Identity Threat Detection & Response (ITDR) — Delivers instant alerts on data theft, account compromise, configuration drift, and anomalous AI agent behavior through hundreds of pre-built detection controls. Responds automatically through integration with existing security tools

At the core of the platform is Reco's Knowledge Graph, which correlates identities, data, applications, AI agents, locations, and access paths into a single contextual model — transforming vast amounts of diverse SaaS data into business context at SaaS speed. Reco AI Agents sit on top of this graph, correlating activity into coherent narratives, recommending remediation steps, and preparing actions for automation — reducing security team workload by up to 80%. The platform is SOC 2 Type II certified and ISO 27001 accredited.

## Outcome
I was genuinely excited about Reco for several reasons:

* Strong founder: Ofer Klein is always very to the point and we developed a great relationship with him throughout the diligence process. His directness, execution speed, and ability to convert strategic introductions into paying enterprise customers stood out as exceptional founder qualities
* Compelling product architecture: Reco's dynamic SaaS security approach — pairing an extensive knowledge graph mapping of third-party services, identities, data flows, and AI agents to provide a real-time understanding of the SaaS application landscape and possible threats — was highly differentiated. The ability to map shadow AI and deliver contextual intelligence across the entire SaaS ecosystem, rather than just monitoring a handful of core applications, positioned Reco well ahead of legacy SSPM vendors
* Exceptional traction: We made a number of introductions to strategic partners, and a significant proportion of them showed strong interest — with Reco able to convert many of them into customers. The company was very quickly ramping and gaining significant enterprise traction, reporting 500% ARR growth in 2024 and an additional 400% growth in 2025, serving multiple Fortune 500 companies across financial services, healthcare, and manufacturing

That said, the partnership ultimately did not invest due to round dynamics. An investor wanted to take a majority of the round, which meant our investment would have been structured as some form of secondary that converted to primary — a structure the partnership did not want to participate in.

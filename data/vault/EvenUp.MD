## Problem
Personal injury law is a massive but structurally broken market where fair outcomes are largely left to chance, and firms lack the data and tools to consistently maximize value for their clients.

* Approximately 20 million Americans are injured each year in vehicle accidents, workplace incidents, or medical malpractice — creating an enormous volume of claims that personal injury firms must process, often with limited staff and outdated workflows
* Over 95% of personal injury cases settle privately, which means there is almost no accessible data on what similar cases actually resolve for. Attorneys are forced to rely on intuition and anecdotal experience when valuing cases, leading to settlements that can vary by multiples for nearly identical injuries
* The demand letter — the critical document that establishes case value and drives settlement negotiations — is extraordinarily labor-intensive to produce, requiring attorneys and paralegals to manually review thousands of pages of medical records, bills, and police reports
* Case timelines routinely stretch beyond a year, creating financial pressure on victims who may accept lowball offers simply because they cannot afford to wait — a dynamic that systematically advantages well-resourced insurance carriers over individual plaintiffs
* Existing legal software is largely focused on practice management and billing, not on the substantive work of case valuation and document preparation. There was no scalable, AI-native solution that could both assess what a case is worth and then help build the argument to get there

## Product
EvenUp provides the Claims Intelligence Platform™, powered by its proprietary Piai™ AI model trained on hundreds of thousands of injury cases and millions of medical records. The platform spans the entire case lifecycle through an integrated suite of products:

* Case Valuation & Settlement Intelligence — Uses predictive machine learning trained on a proprietary dataset of over 250,000 public verdicts and private settlements to estimate the true merit and value of each case. The Settlement Repository™ provides data-driven benchmarks that replace attorney intuition with empirical analysis, fundamentally changing how firms approach negotiations
* AI-Generated Demand Letters — The flagship Demands™ product leverages generative AI to analyze raw case files (medical records, bills, police reports) and produce high-quality, customized demand packages in a fraction of the time and cost of manual drafting. Express Demands™ are generated entirely by AI in minutes; Expert-Reviewed Demands™ are enhanced by an in-house legal team. EvenUp demand letters are approximately 70% more likely to achieve a policy limit settlement
* Medical Management & Case Preparation — Automatically generates medical chronologies (MedChrons™), detects missing bills and records, identifies treatment gaps and prior incidents, and surfaces case strengths and weaknesses — all before a demand is ever written
* Communication Agents™ — AI-powered voice and text assistants that proactively engage with clients, catching issues early in the process. Deployed firms have seen issues flagged in 37% of client conversations
* Negotiation Preparation & Case Companion™ — An AI assistant that helps attorneys anticipate defense arguments, identify surgical indicators and diagnostic details, and anchor higher settlements during mediations and negotiations
* Executive Analytics™ — Firm-level dashboards with real-time performance tracking, demand velocity, treatment continuity, and peer benchmarks against top-performing firms

The platform integrates with leading case management systems and is SOC 2 Type II audited and HIPAA attested. A 300+ person legal operations team — composed of former personal injury attorneys, case managers, and paralegals — continuously trains and reviews the AI models.

## Outcome
The partnership invested in EvenUp at the Series D for several reasons:

* Revenue ramp and unit economics: EvenUp demonstrated a revenue trajectory and unit economics profile that clearly signaled strong product-market fit. Firms using EvenUp were seeing an average revenue lift of over 30%, resolving cases months sooner, and saving hours of legal drafting time per claim — creating tangible, measurable ROI that drove retention and expansion
* Layered AI approach with tangible value delivery: What made EvenUp's product architecture particularly compelling was the combination of "traditional" predictive machine learning for case valuation with advanced generative AI for document drafting. The case valuation capability — predicting the worth of a personal injury case using proprietary settlement data — replaced what had previously been pure lawyer intuition, giving firms a quantitative anchor they never had before. The generative AI layer then automated the labor-intensive work of translating that analysis into persuasive legal arguments. This one-two punch of predictive-then-generative AI delivered value that was immediately recognizable to customers
* Data moat enabling platform expansion: EvenUp's accumulation of hundreds of thousands of personal injury cases and their outcomes constitutes a proprietary dataset that is extraordinarily difficult to replicate and compounds in value with every case processed. With over 95% of PI settlements being private, this data asset creates a structural advantage that clearly supports expansion from demand letters into a full-lifecycle platform for personal injury law firms — which the company has since executed on aggressively
